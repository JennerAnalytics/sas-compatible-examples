# When should Americans buy their Halloween pumpkins?

**Short answer: this weekend. Waiting for a Halloween bargain probably won't pay off, at least not in the grocery-store ads.**

We analyzed 16 seasons of USDA records of advertised supermarket prices for carving pumpkins (2011-2026), and the pattern repeats almost every year:

- Advertised prices peak in mid-September, about 11% above the season's low.
- They bottom out in **early to mid October**, when stores feature pumpkins most heavily. These are also the weeks when Google searches for "pumpkin patch" peak. Stores use pumpkins to bring shoppers in.
- In the **final week before Halloween**, stores largely stop advertising pumpkins. The few ads that remain are priced higher than the mid-October deals.

## The 2026 forecast

- The latest USDA report (ad week 10-16 October) puts the national average advertised price at **$5.36 per pumpkin**.
- Our model expects the cheapest remaining day to be **Saturday 24 October, at about $5.51** (95% prediction interval $4.30-$6.95).
- By **31 October** the expected advertised price rises to about **$6.33** ($4.89-$8.05).
- Across 20,000 simulated price paths, the ad week of **24-30 October** was cheaper than Halloween itself **81%** of the time.

## How sure are we?

- In tests on the 2016-2025 seasons, forecasts made at this point in October missed the actual weekly price by about **40 cents** on average. The 95% ranges contained the actual price in every test week.
- Differences between nearby days are small compared with that uncertainty. The useful message is "buy in the next week rather than waiting", not that one particular day is the cheapest.
- These are **advertised supermarket prices**, not farm-stand or pumpkin-patch prices. Day-to-day prices aren't published (ads run for a week). We also can't see unadvertised last-minute markdowns, so a store may still clear leftover pumpkins cheaply on 31 October. Selection will be thin by then, though.

## What didn't matter

- **Wholesale prices**: day-to-day moves in wholesale pumpkin prices did not help predict supermarket ad prices.
- **Harvest size**: years with bigger national harvests were not noticeably cheaper.
